Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to some of our most common questions. Don't see what you're looking for? Please feel free to reach out and our team will be happy to assist.

What are the differences in memberships?

For more information, please email us at community@eastsidepaddleclub.com.

Do you offer indoor and outdoor courts?

Currently, we have 6 indoor courts and 4 additional rooftop courts projected for March 2025.

Can I rent equipment like paddles and balls?

Yes members can demo paddles for free and we supply the balls.

Do you have a pro shop on-site?

Yes, we have an on-site pro shop where you can buy paddles, accessories, bags, merch, and more

Do you offer lessons or clinics?

Yes we have several experienced coaches who offer lessons and clinics.

Are there any discounts for seniors, students, or military/first responders?

Yes, we provide discounts for seniors, students, military, and first responders! For
more information, email community@eastsidepaddleclub.com.

Do members get priority access to court bookings and events?

Yes members get priority access to all court reservations and free or discounted
events.

How do I book a court?

Members book courts through their court reserve app while nonmembers can book through the website by signing up under the visitor booking membership.

What is the process for participating in open play?

All Open Play sessions must be registered on Court Reserve.

Kings/Queens Court 3:
‍
Paddle Stack of 2, games to 11 win by 2. Winners stay together. If Win three games in a row, then winners come off court. Then next four will play. Anyone coming off court must paddle stack at end of stack.

Advanced Court 4:
‍
2 on and off, Winners Stay and Split UNTIL there are THREE sets of Paddle Stacks of 4, then 4 on and off. Games to 11 Win by 2. When the game is over all four people come off court and the next four go on. Anyone coming off court must paddle stack at the end of stack.
‍
Intermediate Court 5:
‍
Same as court 4. 2 on and off, Winners Stay and Split UNTIL there are THREE sets of Paddle Stacks of 4, then 4 on and off. Games to 11 Win by 2. When the game is over all four people come off court and the next four go on. Anyone coming off court must paddle stack at the end of stack.

Beginner Court 6:
‍
Same as court 4 & 5. 2 on and off, Winners Stay and Split UNTIL there are THREE sets of Paddle Stacks of 4, then 4 on and off. Games to 11 Win by 2. When the game is over all four people come off court and the next four go on. Anyone coming off court must paddle stack at the end of stack.

Will day passes be offered?

Yes, day passes are available at a rate of $20/day. Club members receive two guest passes per month.

How do we connect with other members of ESPC?

We offer a convenient way to connect with fellow members. You can join our TeamReach. For assistance in accessing this platform, feel free to contact our front desk – they're here to help!

You can also join our TeamReach using code: ESPC78702

WIll family plans or corporate membership plans be offered?

Yes! We offer both options. Please visit our memberships section to learn more. For more information, email Kassidy Seebeck.

Do you have coaching & clinics?

We are currently looking for coaches to have 1 on 1’s and to provide clinics.

Can non-members book courts?

Yes, non-members can book courts through our website and by signing up under visitor booking on Court Reserve.

What are the rates for non-members?

Non-members can book courts during off-peak hours (6 AM-4 PM) for $32 and $40 during peak hours (4 PM-Close).

What is open play, and how does it work?

Open Play: A casual, social format where players can join pickup games. Players place their paddles in a queue and rotate onto the court when it's their turn.

Open play is free for members and nonmembers can pay for registered or drop-in open plays.

Registration is recommended, but walk-ins are allowed if space is available.

Drop-In: For players who want to show up and play on any courts (If Available).
